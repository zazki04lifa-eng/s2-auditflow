🚀 HOW IT WORKS:
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. User opens AuditFlow
   ↓
2. Clicks "New Project"
   ↓
3. Sees form + 4 case study cards below
   ↓
4. Clicks any case study card
   ↓
5. System auto-loads:
   - Creates project with all metadata
   - Fills complete business process narrative
   - Sets COSO framework
   - Generates unique project ID
   ↓
6. Toast notification: "Studi Kasus X berhasil dimuat"
   ↓
7. Auto-redirect to Audit Planning Workspace
   ↓
8. User sees Stage 1 (Input) with full narasi already filled
   ↓
9. User can now:
   - Generate Flowchart (Stage 2)
   - Generate Audit Analysis (Stage 3)
   - Review & Export (Stage 4)
